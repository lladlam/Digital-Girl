# DIGITAL GIRL — THE FRAME IS A LIE

An original programmatic animated music video for the supplied Jinja recording.

## Story
The computer is architecture. A model citizen lives inside an immaculate ivory frame. The frame repeats into a city, into an archive, into a cage. A red cursor first assigns her a pose, later threatens to delete her. She discovers a red seam in her own chest. In the last chorus she takes the cursor and turns that seam into a horizon. At the end the apparent outside is revealed to be another window, with a second cursor waiting. The final frame does not fade away.

## Character
One reusable SVG puppet, named DG-01: angular oval face, blunt asymmetric black bob, an ivory forelock, red rectangular hair clip, red irises, mint ear hardware, high black collar, ivory panel on a fitted black tunic, a red seam, pleated skirt, long black boots. Identical proportions in every shot. No external character images and no borrowed character design. Expression follows the dramatic situation; no arbitrary blinking. Default perfect pose → hesitation → reach → defensive curl → grasp → release. Hold a resolved pose between motivated actions.

## Palette
Paper #F0EBDC. Ink #171B1C. Signal red #EF443B. Memory mint #B9D0BF. Dust #B7B2A5. Early scenes use paper and ink with restrained red marks. The first chorus exposes red fields. The instrumental takes away almost all red. The last chorus makes red a deliberate tool. The ending returns to paper with one red pixel.

## Typography
Locally bundled, open licensed fonts: Barlow Condensed 700/800 for physical words; Space Grotesk 500/700 for editorial text; IBM Plex Mono 400 for system inscriptions. No remote font loads. A lyric may form the walls, a command, a floor, a cutout or a full-frame impact. Word arrival, emphasis and transition derive from the supplied word start/end values. No conventional subtitle band. Small technical lettering is texture, never the main plot.

## UI language
Original rectangular architecture, a narrow lintel and one cross. No platform logos, task bars, dashboards or copied desktop chrome. Repeated frames have depth, cast graphic shadows and occlude the character. A pointer is a physical actor. Boundaries contain her until she deliberately crosses them.

## Camera
16:9 1920×1080 at 30fps. Mix of extreme eye closeups, asymmetric portraits, full figures, deep perspective corridors, overhead layouts, a pointer POV and minimal wide shots. Camera changes are motivated by a cut, gesture or crossed threshold. No perpetual push-in. Important facial details generally remain near the middle third, but wides retain purposeful negative space. The vertical edition uses a separate 1080×1920 stage and typography; it is not a center crop.

## Motion rules
MP3 time is the only clock. Every frame is a pure deterministic function of song time. Hard pose holds and 4–10 frame transitions. Mechanical quarter-turns and matched positions early; diagonals and broken constraints after awakening. Beat analysis drives only impacts, not blanket scale pulsing. Low-frequency energy may modify a crack or a light, never every layer.

## Glitch rules
No glitch wallpaper. Brief displaced strips occur only on the first contradiction, the first break and the system's retaliation. Maximum one brief burst per significant event. During her final control the image becomes stable and precise again. Deterministic seeded fragments, never Math.random at render time.

## Editing rules
The opening starts inside an already-open eye, with the cage visible in its iris. Frame → iris → room is a graphic match. Full-line beats determine shot durations; selected word boundaries trigger inserts. A collapsing rectangle becomes the next shot's horizon. The instrumental contains a sustained quiet view. The final chorus changes agency, not just color. Ending holds a question rather than fading to black.

## Lighting and texture
Flat cel shading with sharp offset shadows, restrained paper grain, line hatching and selected flat spot colors. No indiscriminate bloom, chromatic aberration, neon gradients or expensive SVG filter stacks. Contrast comes from cutouts, foreground silhouettes and spare pools of light.

## Forbidden patterns
Random cyberpunk; purple/blue default; stock footage; generated image slideshows; equalizer bars; UI cards as composition; constant hair sway; repeated center staging; text unreadably near an edge; long logo intro; all scenes moving at once; invented lyric timing; a fade-to-black ending.
